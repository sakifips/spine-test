The Spine Test

Assessment of the most likely function attitudes associated with your Hero/Heroine and Anima/Animus Archetypes. Based primarily on the work of Dr. John Beebe.

Instructions

This assessment is based on the 8 function archetypal model of John Beebe. The axis of our relation to self ie the "spine" of our personality refers to the relation between the Hero/Heroine (which carries one's Dominant function attitude) and the Anima/Animus (which carries one's Inferior function).

Choose the answer that feels most instinctive, even if none describes you perfectly. Avoid choosing the answer you think is socially desirable or psychologically safest for you.

Refrain from answering with your ideal self instead of your natural tendencies.
